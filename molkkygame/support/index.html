Support

Answers to common questions about the Fifty Mölkky game. If you do not find yours, write to us.

How do I throw?

Drag down on the playing field and release. The direction of the drag sets where the stick goes and its length sets the power. The dotted line shows the path up to the first hit.

Choose Lob to throw over the pins in front: the ring shows where the stick lands.

How does scoring work?

The scoring follows the rules of Mölkky:

  • If you knock down one pin, you score the number on it. If you knock down two or more, you score the number of pins that fell.
  • Exactly 50 points wins. If you go over 50, your score drops back to 25.
  • Three misses in a row end the game. A scoring throw resets the count.
  • Fallen pins are stood up where they slid to, so every throw changes the field.
Why can I play the daily puzzle only once?

The daily puzzle is the same for everyone, so everyone gets one attempt. Your throws are saved as you play, and the puzzle continues from where you left it if you close the app. Practice and Full game can be played as often as you like.

What is the goal shown in the puzzle?

It is a number of throws that solves the puzzle, found by the app. It is something to aim for, not a limit: a good throw can beat it.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline, including the how-to-play guide. An internet connection is only used to show ads and to check for app updates.

What happens to my results when I change phones?

There is no account and no cloud sync, so the app does not move your data to a new phone by itself. Results, the streak and settings are stored only on the device.

If you restore a full backup of your old phone (iCloud or Google backup), the app’s data may come along, depending on your backup settings. Otherwise you start fresh on the new phone.

How do I change my ad privacy settings?

Open Settings → Ads and privacy in the app. Where consent applies (for example in the EU), there is an Ad privacy settings button that reopens Google’s consent form.

On iPhone you can also change the tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset your advertising ID in your phone’s settings. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your phone. All results, settings and the background picture are removed with it.
