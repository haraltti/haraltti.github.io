How to play

Drag down on the board and let go: the stick-out arrow sets where your bag lands. A Slider lands short and slides on; a Lob drops almost where it lands.

Bag in the hole = 3, bag on the board = 1, off the board = 0. Your score counts every blue bag where it lies, so knocking your own bag in counts too. The red bags are blockers.

Land on exactly 21 in as few bags as you can. Go over and you drop back to 13. Four bags per inning, four innings. Everyone gets the same puzzle each day.
